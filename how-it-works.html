How It Works

From "what's for dinner?" to dinner planned.

Here's exactly how Plate turns your family's real-life constraints into a week of meals you'll actually make.

01
Setup (once)

Tell Plate about your family

The onboarding wizard takes about 5 minutes. It asks everything needed to make sure your weekly menus actually fit your life.

✓Household size and individual family members
✓Dietary restrictions and food allergies, applied strictly
✓Cook time availability by day, including no-cook nights
✓Cuisine preferences: what you love and what you never want
02
Every week

Get your personalized weekly menu

Each week, the AI picks from your creator's recipe library. It balances variety, avoids repeats, and filters for your family's needs so you never have to think about what's for dinner.

✓Recipes filtered at the ingredient level for your restrictions
✓Cook times matched to each day's availability
✓No repeats from the past 3 weeks
✓Variety across cuisines and proteins every week
03
Review and approve

Swap anything. Approve in one tap.

See the full week at a glance. Tap any meal to swap it out. Approve when you're happy: that's when your grocery list is built.

04
Shop

Your grocery list, automatically organized

Every ingredient from every recipe, combined and sorted so you move through the store once, not back and forth.

✓Ingredients aggregated and combined across all recipes
✓Organized by aisle: produce, protein, pantry, dairy
✓Check off items as you go with live progress tracking
✓Instacart one-tap ordering coming soon
Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to download an app?

Nope. Plate is a progressive web app. It works in any browser and you can add it to your home screen without going through the App Store. On iOS, open it in Safari and tap "Add to Home Screen." On Android, tap "Install" when prompted.

How does Plate know which recipes to use?

Your meal planning app is powered by the recipe library of the food creator whose app you're using. The AI selects from their recipes, not a generic database. That's what makes the menus feel like they came from someone who actually cooks the way you do.

What if my family has multiple dietary restrictions?

Plate handles complex combinations: multiple allergies, lifestyle diets, and per-family-member restrictions all at once. Restrictions are applied at the ingredient level. If your household is gluten-free plus nut-allergy, you'll only ever see recipes that meet both conditions.

What happens to nights I don't want to cook?

During onboarding, you tell Plate which nights are no-cook nights. Those days are skipped in menu generation. No recipe is assigned, no ingredients added to the grocery list. Pizza Friday stays pizza Friday.

Is Plate free to use?

The base meal planning experience is free. Your creator may also offer premium recipe packs as optional purchases inside the app.
