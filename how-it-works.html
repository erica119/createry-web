How it works

From your recipe to their dinner table.

Creators bring the food. Createry connects it to each household's real week, from planning through shopping.

For creators

Build a home for your recipes.

Set up your creator presence, bring in your recipe library, and share your Createry experience with your audience.

01

Shape your presence

Add your name, logo, and brand colors.

02

Bring your recipes

Add them manually, import from a website URL, or use a CSV for a larger library. Check ingredients before sharing so shopping lists are useful.

03

Invite your audience

Give followers a place to plan with your food, beyond a single post or saved link.

For households

One week, built around real life.

1. Tell us about the household

Set the people, preferences, dietary needs, and shape of the week.

2. Generate a menu

Build a weekly plan using recipes from the creator's library.

3. Edit before approving

Review the schedule, swap meals, and adjust the plan.

4. Make the shopping list

Turn approved recipes into ingredients to check off or send to Instacart.

Availability and item matching in Instacart depend on the selected retailer and location. Review the cart before checkout.

The complete loop

Content becomes a habit.

The goal is simple: make your recipes useful at the exact moment a household decides what to cook and what to buy.
