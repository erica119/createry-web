How It Works

Three steps to a week of dinners you'll actually want to make

1

Tell us about your family

Share household size, dietary restrictions, cook time per day, and the cuisines you love. Takes 5 minutes.

2

Get your weekly menu

AI picks from your creator's recipe library so nothing gets overused, variety stays balanced, and you never have to decide what's for dinner.

3

Shop with ease

Your grocery list is automatically organized by aisle. Instacart integration coming soon makes it one tap to order.

Features

Meal planning that actually knows your family

This isn't a generic weekly menu. It's built around your real life: picky eaters, busy weeknights, no-cook Fridays, and all.

🧠

Smart picks from recipes you already trust

The AI selects from your creator's recipe library every week, making sure meals stay varied, nothing gets repeated too soon, and the "what's for dinner?" decision is made for you.

🚫

Dietary filters that actually work

Allergies and preferences applied at the ingredient level. Nut allergy? You'll never see a recipe with tree nuts. Ever.

⏱️

Cook time matched to each day

30 minutes on Monday, all afternoon on Saturday. Plate knows the difference, including nights you don't cook at all.

🛒

Shopping list, built automatically

All ingredients aggregated, combined, and organized by aisle. One list, no duplicates, ready the moment you approve your menu.
