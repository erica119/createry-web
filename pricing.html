Questions

Pricing questions answered

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

Yes. You'll enter your payment details when you sign up, but you won't be charged until your 7-day trial ends. Cancel before day 8 and you pay nothing.

Can I switch from monthly to annual?

Yes, anytime. You can upgrade to annual from your billing settings and we'll prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

What happens if I cancel?

Your account stays active until the end of your current billing period. After that, your branded app goes offline and your audience loses access. Your recipe data is retained for 30 days in case you reactivate.

Are there any transaction fees on top of the platform fee?

Createry retains 20% of recipe pack sales and user subscriptions as a platform fee. Standard Stripe processing fees also apply to each transaction. There are no other hidden fees.

What does my audience pay?

Your users subscribe at $9/month or $90/year to access your branded app. They also have the option to purchase any premium recipe packs you choose to offer, at prices you set.

Will the price increase over time?

Annual plan subscribers lock in their rate for the full year. Monthly subscribers will receive 30 days notice before any price changes. Early creator partners get priority consideration for locked rates.
