Questions

What the numbers mean.

Does 200,000 followers mean 20,000 subscribers?

Only at a 10% conversion assumption. At 1%, that audience would yield 2,000 paying subscribers. You can test either scenario above.

Is the 80% based on all revenue?

The calculator applies 80% to the $9 monthly audience subscription. It does not count recipe pack purchases or Instacart transactions.

What about annual subscriptions?

The calculator models monthly subscriptions only. Annual billing changes when money is received and how a monthly equivalent is presented.

What is excluded?

Payment processing fees, refunds, taxes, and other business costs. The calculator separately subtracts the $199 monthly creator platform fee.
